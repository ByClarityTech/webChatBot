# Frequently Asked Questions

## What industries do you support?

We work with SaaS companies, professional services firms, and internal IT teams that need grounded AI assistants over their own documentation.

## How long does a typical project take?

A focused public-docs chatbot MVP usually ships in 4–6 weeks. Larger multi-agent platforms take 8–12 weeks depending on integrations.

## Do you host the chatbot?

Yes. We can host the API and widget for you, or deploy into your cloud account (AWS, Azure, GCP) with infrastructure-as-code.

## What file formats can the assistant learn from?

PDF, DOCX, PPTX, HTML, Markdown, and plain text files are supported through our Docling-based ingestion pipeline.

## Is conversation history stored?

Yes. Multi-turn chats use a thread ID backed by PostgreSQL so visitors can ask follow-up questions in the same session.
